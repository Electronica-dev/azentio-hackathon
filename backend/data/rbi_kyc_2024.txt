Master Direction – Know Your Customer (KYC) Direction, 2016
(Updated as on November 8, 2024)

12. Simplified measures for low risk customers
(a) Regulated Entities may apply simplified KYC measures for customers assessed as low risk.
(b) A customer assessed as low risk may open an account with an aggregate balance not exceeding fifty thousand rupees on the basis of a self-declaration and one officially valid document.
(c) The simplified measures shall not apply where the customer is a politically exposed person.

16. Periodic updation of KYC
(1) Every Regulated Entity shall carry out periodic updation of KYC records at the following intervals:
(a) high-risk customers — at least once every twenty-four months;
(b) medium-risk customers — at least once every thirty-six months;
(c) low-risk customers — at least once every sixty months.
(2) The risk categorisation of every customer shall be reviewed at least once every twelve months.
(3) Where a customer fails to submit the required documents within thirty days of a request, the Regulated Entity shall suspend operations in the account until the records are updated.

17. Beneficial ownership
(3) For the purposes of this Direction, beneficial owner means an individual who ultimately owns or controls a customer and includes:
(a) in the case of a company, the natural person who owns or controls more than twenty-five per cent of the shares or voting rights;
(b) in the case of a partnership firm, the natural person who owns or controls more than twenty-five per cent of the capital or profits;
(c) in the case of a trust, the author, the trustee and the beneficiary, where the trust holds more than twenty-five per cent of the shares or capital.
The Regulated Entity shall identify and verify every beneficial owner before onboarding.

19. Video based customer identification process
(2) The video based customer identification process shall be conducted by an official of the Regulated Entity who is authorised for this purpose.
(3) The Regulated Entity shall retain the video recording for a period of five years.
(4) The Regulated Entity may use the services of a third party for the video based customer identification process.

35. Record management
(1) Every Regulated Entity shall maintain records of transactions and of the identity of customers for a period of five years after the business relationship has ended.
(2) The records shall be maintained in a manner that permits retrieval within a reasonable period.
