Guidelines on Responsible Use of Artificial Intelligence
in Credit Decisioning

1. Short title and commencement
(1) These Guidelines may be called the Reserve Bank of India (Responsible Use of Artificial Intelligence in Credit Decisioning) Guidelines, 2026.
(2) They shall come into force with effect from January 15, 2027.

2. Applicability
(1) These Guidelines shall apply to every Regulated Entity that uses, or proposes to use, an artificial intelligence or machine learning model to assess creditworthiness, set a credit limit or price a loan.
(2) The Board of the Regulated Entity shall approve an AI governance policy before any such model is placed into production.

3. Explainability of automated credit decisions
(1) Every Regulated Entity shall be able to explain, in simple language and within seven days of a request, the principal factors that influenced an automated credit decision.
(2) The Regulated Entity shall maintain a model card for every model in production describing its purpose, training data, limitations and known failure modes.

4. Model validation and monitoring
(1) Every Regulated Entity shall validate each credit model before deployment and at least once every twelve months thereafter.
(2) The Regulated Entity shall monitor model performance and bias indicators at least once every quarter and shall report material deterioration to the Board.

5. Third-party and vendor models
(1) A Regulated Entity shall not deploy a third-party AI model for credit decisioning unless the vendor discloses the model's training approach and the Regulated Entity completes a due diligence assessment.
(2) The Regulated Entity shall maintain a register of all AI vendors and models used in credit decisioning.

6. Grievance redressal and audit trail
(1) Every Regulated Entity shall provide a grievance channel for customers who believe an automated decision was unfair and shall resolve complaints within thirty days.
(2) The Regulated Entity shall retain the inputs, outputs and version identifier of every automated credit decision for a period of eight years.
