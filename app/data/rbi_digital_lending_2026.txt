Digital Lending Directions, 2025
Amendment – 2026

1. Short title and commencement
(1) This Direction may be called the Digital Lending (Amendment) Direction, 2026.
(2) It shall come into force with effect from January 15, 2027.

2. Amendment to Section 7 — default loss guarantee
In Section 7 of the Directions, for the words "five per cent", the words "ten per cent" shall be substituted.
The enhanced limit shall apply only where the lending service provider has been subjected to an annual audit of its recovery practices.

3. Insertion of new Section 9A — key fact statement
After Section 9 of the Directions, the following section shall be inserted, namely:
9A. Key fact statement
(1) Every Regulated Entity shall provide a key fact statement to the borrower in English and in the language of the State where the borrower resides.
(2) The key fact statement shall include the annual percentage rate, the total cost of credit, the recovery agent details and the grievance redressal mechanism.
(3) The borrower shall acknowledge receipt of the key fact statement through an explicit action, and the acknowledgement shall be retained for eight years.

4. Amendment to Section 12 — cooling off period
In Section 12 of the Directions, for the words "three days", the words "seven days" shall be substituted.
During the cooling off period the borrower shall be permitted to exit the loan by repaying the principal and the proportionate interest without any penalty.

5. Insertion of new Section 15A — reporting of lending service providers
After Section 15 of the Directions, the following section shall be inserted, namely:
15A. Reporting of lending service providers
(1) Every Regulated Entity shall report the onboarding, termination and material change in the ownership of a lending service provider to the Credit Information Companies within fifteen calendar days.
(2) The Regulated Entity shall conduct an annual information security audit of every lending service provider and shall report the findings to the Board.
