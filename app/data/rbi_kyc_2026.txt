Master Direction – Know Your Customer (KYC) Direction, 2016
Amendment – 2026

1. Short title and commencement
(1) This Direction may be called the Know Your Customer (KYC) (Amendment) Direction, 2026.
(2) It shall come into force with effect from December 1, 2026.

2. Amendment to Section 16 — periodic updation of KYC
In the Master Direction, for Section 16, the following section shall be substituted, namely:
16. Periodic updation of KYC
(1) Every Regulated Entity shall carry out periodic updation of KYC records at the following intervals:
(a) high-risk customers — at least once every twelve months;
(b) medium-risk customers — at least once every twenty-four months;
(c) low-risk customers — at least once every thirty-six months.
(2) The risk categorisation of every customer shall be reviewed on the occurrence of a trigger event and in any case not less than once every six months.
(3) Where a customer fails to submit the required documents within thirty days of a request, the Regulated Entity shall suspend operations in the account until the records are updated.

3. Amendment to Section 17 — beneficial ownership
In the Master Direction, for sub-section (3) of Section 17, the following shall be substituted, namely:
17(3) For the purposes of this Direction, beneficial owner means an individual who ultimately owns or controls a customer and includes:
(a) in the case of a company, the natural person who owns or controls more than ten per cent of the shares or voting rights;
(b) in the case of a partnership firm, the natural person who owns or controls more than ten per cent of the capital or profits;
(c) in the case of a trust, the author, the trustee and the beneficiary, where the trust holds more than ten per cent of the shares or capital.
The Regulated Entity shall identify and verify every beneficial owner before onboarding and shall refresh the ownership determination at least once every twelve months.

4. Insertion of new Section 18A — verification of PAN and Aadhaar
After Section 18 of the Master Direction, the following section shall be inserted, namely:
18A. Verification of PAN and Aadhaar
(1) Every Regulated Entity shall, at the time of onboarding and before every high-value transaction exceeding rupees ten lakh, verify that the Permanent Account Number of the customer is active and linked with Aadhaar.
(2) Where the verification fails, the Regulated Entity shall not commence or continue the relationship and shall record the failure in the exception register within twenty-four hours.
(3) The exception register shall be reviewed by the Principal Officer every month.

5. Amendment to Section 19 — video based customer identification
In Section 19 of the Master Direction, after sub-section (2), the following shall be inserted, namely:
19(2A) The video based customer identification process shall include liveness detection and geo-tagging of the customer at the time of the interaction. The Regulated Entity shall retain the video recording for a period of eight years.
19(2B) The Regulated Entity shall not outsource the liveness detection function to a third party without the prior approval of the Board.

6. Insertion of new Section 22A — reporting of KYC exceptions
After Section 22 of the Master Direction, the following section shall be inserted, namely:
22A. Reporting of KYC exceptions to CRILC
(1) Every Regulated Entity shall report KYC exception cases, including failed PAN-Aadhaar verification and incomplete beneficial ownership records, to the Central Repository of Information on Large Credits within seven calendar days of identification.
(2) The report shall be approved by the Principal Officer and shall be retained for a period of eight years.
(3) The Board shall be informed of the number and nature of exceptions every quarter.

7. Amendment to Section 35 — record management
In Section 35 of the Master Direction, for the words "five years", the words "eight years" shall be substituted.
The records referred to in sub-section (1) shall be maintained in a manner that permits retrieval within forty-eight hours of a request from the supervisor.

8. Insertion of new Section 38A — consent based data sharing
After Section 38 of the Master Direction, the following section shall be inserted, namely:
38A. Consent based data sharing
(1) Every Regulated Entity shall obtain explicit, purpose-bound and revocable consent from the customer before sharing KYC data with any third party, including an account aggregator.
(2) The Regulated Entity shall maintain an immutable audit trail of every consent, including the purpose, the recipient and the time of revocation, and shall produce it within forty-eight hours of a request.
(3) Consent shall not be obtained as a condition for availing any basic banking service.

9. Amendment to Section 42 — board approved policy
In Section 42 of the Master Direction, the following shall be inserted, namely:
42(4) The KYC policy shall be reviewed by the Board at least once every six months.
42(5) The Audit Committee shall review KYC exceptions and the status of periodic updation every quarter and shall place its findings before the Board.

10. Omission of Clause 12(b)
Clause 12(b) of the Master Direction, which permitted simplified KYC measures for low-risk customers opening accounts with an aggregate balance not exceeding fifty thousand rupees, is omitted.
Consequent to the omission, every customer shall be subject to full KYC verification irrespective of the balance maintained in the account.
